My Story & Mission

From People-Pleasing to Emotional Sovereignty

For many years, I believed that love meant self-sacrifice. I put others first, ignored red flags, and convinced myself that if I only tried harder, things would improve.

When I finally broke the toxic cycle, I dedicated my professional life to mastering the psychological tools of emotional detachment, boundary setting, and identity reclamation.

Today, having coached over 500+ individuals, my mission is simple: to ensure you never have to diminish your worth to keep someone else comfortable.
